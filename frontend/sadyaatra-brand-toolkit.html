01

Overview

Sadyaatra exists to take the friction out of travel planning. The identity should feel the same way: grounded, warm, and unfussy — never loud, never corporate.

"Effortless isn't the absence of detail — it's detail you never have to think about."

Every application of this identity — the packed suitcase-and-wings mark, the earthy palette, the quiet serif wordmark — should read as a well-worn travel companion, not a startup logo. Use this toolkit to keep that feeling consistent everywhere the brand shows up.

Grounded — earthy, natural tones
Unhurried — generous space, no clutter
Warm — human, never sterile
Dependable — clear, legible, consistent
02

Logo System

The primary lockup pairs the suitcase-and-wings mark with the Sadyaatra wordmark and tagline. It works as a full lockup or as the mark alone once the brand is established.

Clear space

Keep clear space equal to the height of the suitcase icon (X) on all sides. Nothing — text, edges, other marks — should enter this zone.

Minimum size
Print — 25mm wide minimum
Digital — 96px wide minimum
Favicon / avatar — icon mark only
Usage rules
  • Always reproduce the wordmark in the approved sage or charcoal — never recolor to match a campaign.
  • On photography or busy backgrounds, place the logo inside a solid clear-space panel.
  • The icon mark may be used alone once the brand is recognizable; the wordmark should not be used without the icon.
  • Reverse the whole lockup to white on dark or saturated backgrounds — never a partial reverse.

Pairing rules
  • On light backgrounds, set body copy in Charcoal (#2B2728), never in a pale tone — keep contrast honest.
  • On dark backgrounds, set body copy in Cream (#DED7CB); reserve Sage Muted for links and interactive accents.
  • Khaki Pale and Blush Grey are backgrounds, not text colors — contrast against white or charcoal is too low.
  • Use no more than one accent color per screen at full saturation; let the neutrals carry the rest.

05

Voice & Tone

Sadyaatra sounds like a well-traveled friend giving directions — clear, reassuring, never salesy.

We sound like this

Plain, warm, and specific — short sentences that tell you exactly what's handled.

Confident without hype: we state what's true rather than what sounds impressive.

Not like this

Exclamation-heavy, urgency-driven "book now!!" sales copy.

Jargon like "synergized itineraries" or "best-in-class travel solutions."

"Your whole trip, packed into one plan."
"We handle the layovers. You handle the memories."
"Nothing to chase. Nothing to double-check."
